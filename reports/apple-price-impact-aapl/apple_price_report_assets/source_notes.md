# Apple Price Impact Report Source Notes

## Report Spine

- Question: Apple raised Mac and iPad prices in June 2026. What does that imply for Apple business quality and AAPL stock over the next 1-3 years?
- Audience: AAPL holder evaluating whether to keep holding.
- Decision-useful answer: Keep a core holding under the base case, but track iPhone pricing, product gross margin, Services growth, and FY27 EPS revisions as explicit triggers.
- Time window: 1-3 years, with near-term monitoring around Q3 FY26 earnings and the fall iPhone cycle.

## Source Inventory

- Apple Q2 FY26 earnings release and Form 10-Q: segment revenue, gross margin, buyback context.
- MacRumors June 25, 2026 price list: affected products and listed price changes.
- MacRumors follow-up on Apple explanation: memory and SSD cost pressure as the stated cause.
- Gartner February 26, 2026 memory-cost forecast: cost inflation and device shipment/pricing pressure.
- MarketWatch quote and analyst estimates pages: latest AAPL quote, market cap, P/E, FY26-FY28 EPS consensus.

## Assumptions

- Scenario ranges are illustrative, not a target price.
- Current price is approximated from publicly available quote data at report creation time.
- Product-level price list uses U.S. listed starting prices reported by MacRumors.
- Apple no longer reports product unit shipments, so the report treats unit demand as a monitoring trigger rather than a modeled actual.

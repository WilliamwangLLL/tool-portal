# Musk Twitter/X Reform Report - Source Notes

Prepared June 28, 2026. X is privately held, so post-2022 financial data is incomplete and not directly comparable to Twitter's public-company filings.

## Core data used in charts

- 2021 Twitter advertising revenue: $4.505B; total revenue: $5.077B. Source: Twitter 2021 Form 10-K, SEC.
- 2021 Twitter headcount baseline: more than 7,500 employees. Source: Twitter 2021 Form 10-K, SEC.
- April 2023 workforce marker: roughly 1,500 employees after layoffs, reported from Elon Musk's BBC interview coverage. Source: Business Insider.
- X advertising revenue: 2023 $2.3B, 2024 $1.7B, 2025 $1.8B. Source: Business Insider report citing SpaceX S-1 disclosures.
- Fidelity mark: 71.5% markdown versus original X share valuation in November 2023 disclosure. Source: TechCrunch.
- Reported valuation rebound: X reportedly returned near $44B valuation in a 2025 secondary share sale; Fidelity had marked X near $10B by late September 2024. Source: The Guardian.
- xAI/X all-stock deal: X valued around $33B equity value / $45B enterprise value. Source: Investopedia and Axios coverage of Elon Musk's March 2025 announcement.
- X debt: Morgan Stanley set to sell $5.5B of X debt at 97 cents on the dollar. Source: Bloomberg Law.
